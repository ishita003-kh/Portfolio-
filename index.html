I'm Ishita — a Computer Science graduate who got interested in AI not through the hype cycle, but through the unglamorous work of checking whether it actually works. At Ethara AI, I spent months looking at model outputs across text, audio, and video, writing up exactly where and why they broke down.

That evaluation mindset carries into how I build. My RAG project, DocuBot, wasn't really about wiring up LangChain and FAISS — the harder part was getting the retrieval grounded enough that the model would rather say "I don't know" than guess. I care more about a system being honest about its limits than about it looking impressive in a demo.

Outside of evaluation and RAG pipelines, I've built dashboards for government stakeholders, published research combining quantum computing with machine learning, and I'm generally happiest when a problem has a clear, structured shape I can pull apart.
